\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Test-Time Adaptation}
\label{sec:related:tta}

Test-Time Adaptation adapts a source-pretrained model to a shifting target distribution using only unlabeled test data. \emph{Normalization-based} methods~\citep{schneider2020adabn,nado2020adabn} recompute batch statistics---efficient but limited in expressiveness. \emph{Entropy-based} methods minimize softmax entropy: Tent~\citep{wang2021tent} updates normalization affine parameters, but is prone to confirmation bias~\citep{niu2022eata,mummadi2021test}; EATA~\citep{niu2022eata} adds reliable sample weighting and Fisher regularization; SAR~\citep{niu2023sar} uses Sharpness-Aware Minimization. \emph{Augmentation-based} methods include TTT~\citep{sun2020ttt}, MEMO~\citep{zhang2022memo}, and CoTTA~\citep{wang2022cotta} (teacher-student with stochastic restore). For ViT~\citep{dosovitskiy2021vit}, LayerNorm-only adaptation is standard~\citep{niu2023sar,come2024come}.

\subsection{Uncertainty, Calibration, and Pseudo-Labeling}
\label{sec:related:uncertainty}

UCDPA relies on \emph{evidential deep learning} (EDL)~\citep{sensoy2018evidential,amini2020evidential}, which places a Dirichlet distribution over class probabilities in a single forward pass, yielding belief masses and an uncertainty mass. Unlike Bayesian neural networks~\citep{kendall2017uncertainties,abdar2021review} and MC Dropout~\citep{gal2016dropout}, EDL is suitable for online TTA. COME~\citep{come2024come} adopts EDL inside TTA, which we extend. Post-hoc calibration methods (temperature scaling~\citep{guo2017calibration}, Platt scaling~\citep{platt1999probabilistic}) require labeled sets unavailable in TTA; our regularizer operates during adaptation without labels (Proposition~\ref{prop:cal}). Pseudo-labeling underpins SSL~\citep{sohn2020fixmatch,xie2020selftraining,lee2013pseudo} and TTA~\citep{prabhu2023lada,chen2023minimize}, but risks confirmation bias. Selective pseudo-labeling via confidence thresholds is well-established~\citep{niu2022eata}; UCDPA's UCB routing extends this by using a \emph{product} of two sigmoids (confidence \emph{and} evidential certainty) and routing \emph{softly} rather than filtering hard. The product form is critical: a confidently-wrong sample (high $c$, high $u$) passes a confidence-only filter but is correctly routed to the conservative path by the UCB product.

\subsection{COME and Recent TTA Paradigms}
\label{sec:related:come}

COME~\citep{come2024come} is our primary baseline. It computes evidential quantities from logits via $p$-norm normalization, forms Dirichlet evidence, and defines an opinion entropy. The conservative entropy loss blends softmax and opinion entropy. UCDPA retains COME's evidential machinery as Path 1, but addresses its three limitations: (i) we route samples rather than treating them uniformly, (ii) we add a pseudo-label cross-entropy path, and (iii) we add a calibration regularizer (Proposition~\ref{prop:cal}).

\paragraph{Comparison with CoTTA, MEMO, POEM, FOA, and STAMP.}
We compare empirically with CoTTA~\citep{wang2022cotta}, MEMO~\citep{zhang2022memo}, POEM~\citep{yi2025poem}, and FOA~\citep{niu2024foa} on ImageNet-C/ViT-B/16 (severity 3, seed 0, mean over 19 corruptions; Table~\ref{tab:cross-backbone}). UCDPA achieves \vitUcdpaAcc\% accuracy, outperforming MEMO (\memoAcc\%, $+3.51$~pp), POEM (\poemAcc\%, $+4.08$~pp), CoTTA (\cottaAcc\%, $+7.39$~pp), and FOA (\foaAcc\%, $+11.42$~pp). POEM, which explores potentially reliable samples via entropy-thresholded selection, underperforms even COME (\vitComeAcc\%), confirming that the evidential uncertainty in COME/UCDPA provides a stronger routing signal than plain softmax entropy. FOA, a derivative-free prompt optimization method, achieves accuracy comparable to the source model (\foaAcc\% vs.\ \vitSourceAcc\%), suggesting that forward-only adaptation provides no meaningful benefit on severe corruptions. CoTTA's teacher-student scheme also underperforms COME, likely because its augmentation-heavy approach is less effective for ViT LayerNorm-only adaptation. STAMP~\citep{prabhu2023stamp} (memory bank with class-balanced replay) is not evaluated but trades accuracy for $K\times$ memory cost. UCDPA's advantage: it is \emph{single-model, single-forward-pass}, its diversification comes from \emph{dual-path routing} rather than augmentation/replay, and it explicitly couples softmax and evidential uncertainty---which CoTTA, MEMO, POEM, and FOA do not.
